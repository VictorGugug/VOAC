# MIRACLE HALL

## English

A website about **Tally Hall**, **Miracle Musical** (ミラクルミュージカル) and **Cojum Dip** — three connected projects from the Ann Arbor music scene.

- **Tally Hall** (2002–2011): indie rock band, known for their colored neckties and the albums *Marvin's Marvelous Mechanical Museum* and *Good & Evil*.
- **Miracle Musical**: Joe Hawley's project, creators of the conceptual album *Hawaii: Part II* (2012).
- **Cojum Dip**: Bora Karaca's avant-garde metal project, with a self-titled album (2014).

Static site — open `index.html` in a browser or serve the folder with any static server.

Una página sobre **Tally Hall**, **Miracle Musical** (ミラクルミュージカル) y **Cojum Dip** — tres proyectos conectados de la escena musical de Ann Arbor.

- **Tally Hall** (2002–2011): banda de indie rock, conocida por sus corbatas de colores y los álbumes *Marvin's Marvelous Mechanical Museum* y *Good & Evil*.
